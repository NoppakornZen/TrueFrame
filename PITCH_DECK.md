## Slide 7: The Decision Engine

### Intelligent Policy Evaluation

**Four-Step Analysis:**

1. **Evidence Validation**
   - Check all required evidence types present
   - Verify AI confidence thresholds

2. **Policy Checking**
   - Serial Number Verification (95% threshold)
   - Certificate of Conformity (90% threshold)
   - Physical Condition (85% threshold)
   - Packaging Integrity (90% threshold)

3. **Conflict Detection**
   - Serial number mismatches
   - Document inconsistencies
   - Contradictory findings

4. **Next Best Evidence**
   - Recommends optimal next upload
   - Prioritizes by impact
